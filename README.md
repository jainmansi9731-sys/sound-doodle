# Sound Doodle

A single-page toy for turning drawings into music, right in the browser.

Draw a line on the canvas — its height becomes pitch (high on the canvas = high pitch, low = deep bass), and its color picks the instrument's timbre. Press **Play** and watch your doodle play itself back.

## Features

- **Live sound** — hear notes as you draw
- **5 instrument colors**, each with its own waveform and octave
- Adjustable **brush size** and **playback tempo**
- **Loop** playback
- **Save PNG** to export your doodle

## Usage

Just open [`index.html`](index.html) in a modern browser — no build step or dependencies required.
